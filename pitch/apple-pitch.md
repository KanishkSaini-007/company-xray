# Acquire Apple (AAPL) - the case built on Wearables

**Target:** Apple Inc. (Nasdaq: AAPL)
**Price:** ~$344 (Oct 16, 2026), market cap ~$5T
**The weak spot:** Wearables, Home and Accessories - $35.7B of FY2025 revenue (8.6%), the ONLY Apple category that shrank (-4% YoY) while everything else grew.

## The pitch in 5 bullets
1. The franchise: iPhone ($209.6B, +4%) plus Services ($109.2B, +14%) is the most profitable hardware-plus-subscription engine ever built. Mac +12%, iPad +5%. The core is compounding.
2. The weak spot is fixable: Wearables shrank not because demand died but because the category has no new must-have feature and AirPods/Watch are priced like accessories. The installed base (over a billion iPhones) hasn't been monetized on health.
3. What the buyer changes: (a) turn Watch health features into a paid Health+ subscription bundled with Services, (b) reprice the Watch lineup to open a mid-tier, (c) pull the home products (HomePod, TV) into one bundle instead of three orphans.
4. Why now: the market prices Apple on iPhone cycle fear; a -4% drag from Wearables is small enough to ignore but big enough to re-rate the story if fixed - every point of Services mix already moves the multiple.
5. The math: if Wearables just returns to flat (0% instead of -4%) that's ~$1.4B of recovered revenue; attach a Health+ subscription at even 5% of Watch owners and it's a new high-margin Services line on top.

## Risks
- At ~$5T market cap this is a thought exercise for a consortium - treat it as learning the framework, not a realistic LBO.
- Services growth depends on the Google search deal, which is under regulatory pressure.
- Health features face slow regulatory approval cycles.
