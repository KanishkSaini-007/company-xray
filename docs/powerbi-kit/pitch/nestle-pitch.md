# Acquire Nestle (NESN.SW) - the case built on Prepared Dishes

**Target:** Nestle S.A. (SIX: NESN)
**Price:** ~CHF 82.4 (Oct 16, 2026)
**The weak spot:** Prepared dishes and cooking aids - CHF 10.1B of 2025 sales, the ONLY product group with negative organic growth (-0.4%), with real internal growth -0.2%. People are quietly buying less frozen/prepared food.

## The pitch in 5 bullets
1. The franchise: Powdered & Liquid Beverages (CHF 25.1B, +7.3% organic) and PetCare (CHF 18.4B, +2.2%, 21.7% margin) are global category leaders with pricing power - coffee and pet food grow through every cycle.
2. The weak spot is fixable: Prepared dishes' decline is a portfolio problem, not a demand collapse - Nestle underinvested there while pushing coffee and pet care. The segment still runs a 19.5% operating margin, so it throws off cash while shrinking.
3. What the buyer changes: (a) divest or consolidate the weakest prepared-food brands and keep the winners (b) shift the freed shelf and marketing budget into PetCare and beverages, (c) fix confectionery's volume problem (-0.7% RIG behind +8.8% pricing - price hikes are masking shrinking volumes).
4. Why now: total group sales fell from CHF 91.4B to CHF 89.5B in 2025 - the market already knows there's a portfolio problem and the stock trades at a discount to its own history. The fix is visible and mechanical.
5. The math: cutting one -0.4% growth, 10%-of-sales segment and redeploying into categories growing 2-7% lifts group organic growth by roughly half a point and removes the biggest drag on the story.

## Risks
- Confectionery's "growth" is all pricing (+8.8% price, -0.7% volume) - that's a second weak spot hiding in a green number.
- Coffee pricing gains followed input-cost inflation; if coffee prices fall, growth optics reverse.
- Swiss large-cap acquisitions face political and regulatory headwinds.
